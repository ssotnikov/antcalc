Antenna Calculator helps you estimate the initial dimensions of a quarter-wave monopole or half-wave dipole and adjust an antenna using its measured resonant frequency.

Enter a frequency and shortening factor to see the wavelength, ideal dimensions and practical length estimates. For tuning, enter the actual antenna length, measured resonance and target frequency. For a dipole, the app shows the total length and the length of each arm.

Features:
• Antenna projects, tuning journal, measurement comparison and JSON transfer
• Cable electrical length, phase and delay using its velocity factor
• SWR, reflection and return loss, including calculations from R + jX
• Frequency units: kHz, MHz and GHz, with 1 Hz resolution
• Length units: mm, cm and m
• Separate inputs for sizing and tuning
• English and Russian interfaces
• Light, dark and system themes
• Bottom navigation and left/right swipes
• Copy results together with their inputs and units
• Built-in diagrams, formulas and reference material

Calculations, help and locally saved settings work offline. Reference links open in an external browser only when you tap them. There are no ads, analytics, user accounts or in-app payments.

These calculations provide starting dimensions for construction and measurement. Conductor diameter, insulation, nearby objects, radials and matching networks need separate consideration. The app does not replace resonance and impedance measurements.

Requires Android 8.0 or later.
